## Session 2

### What We Did

- Defined the current physical design around a layered buoy structure.
- Decided the bottom layer will hold the propellers, underwater instruments, and drive-transfer hardware.
- Decided the motors should stay above the waterline.
- Chose a three-propeller triangular layout with the propellers spaced 120 degrees from each other.
- Updated the physical design document so Fusion modeling can start from a clear baseline.

### Completed Tasks

- [x] Define the first layered physical-design concept
- [x] Record the triangular three-propeller layout idea

### Meeting Notes

- The layered approach is mainly intended to protect the motors and electronics by keeping sensitive hardware above the waterline.
- The next physical design challenge is figuring out the shaft, gear, or transmission path from the upper motor layer to the lower propeller layer.
- Stability, sealing, and drag are now key physical design questions.

## Session 3

- Chose pool noodles as the flotation method for the first prototype.
- Defined the top layer as the location for the GPS antenna, optional Bluetooth antenna, and solar panel.
- Updated the physical design document so the Fusion model can be organized by bottom, middle, and top layers.

### Completed Tasks

- [x] Define the top-layer antenna and solar concept

### Meeting Notes

- The next important physical questions are attachment methods, sealing around shaft paths, and top-heaviness from the solar panel.
