#### Mission start failure — onboard evidence

- The SD system log `logs/system-20261108-5ebd29d6.csv` records nine `MISSION START` events for mission `C92077C9-49E1-4BEB-A347-CF04EA85D6F4`, followed immediately by `MISSION STOP ... reason=HEADING_UNAVAILABLE` each time. The failed attempts correspond to sessions 008–013 and 016–018. These were brief starts followed by an automatic stop, not a mission that ran its route.
- The SD card also contains `/config/navigation.cfg` with a valid `MAG_CAL` record (`-107,-607,1.018572,0.982093`), so a magnetometer calibration was present. The system log records an ELRS calibration request at 22:03:49 UTC blocked with `SCIENCE_IDLE=0`; firmware routes this through its ELRS calibration/pulse routine, so it was not a magnetometer calibration attempt and is unrelated to the mission stops.
- Separate `MISSION ARM BLOCKED` records say `CYCLE ARM LOW; POWER MINIMUM; STICKS CENTERED`, indicating the arm-cycle condition also sometimes was not satisfied. That is a distinct input interlock; it is not the recorded reason for the nine mission stops.
- The earlier hypothesis about being outside the mission home radius came from station-side data and is withdrawn. The onboard logs identify heading availability as the direct mission-stop cause. Firmware review shows the mission start checks accepted the calibrated heading, but the QMC5883 read returns false when a sample is not ready; mission navigation polls every 200 ms and currently treats any failed heading read as fatal. This is a plausible transient-sample race, not proven by the event log because heading samples/failure detail were not recorded. A bounded 500 ms cache of the last valid heading was added: it tolerates brief not-ready samples while still stopping when calibration is missing, the sensor reports an I2C failure, or the cached sample ages out.

### Meeting Notes

- The water-test firmware required the buoy within 5 m of the mission file's fixed Home. SA/CH9 captured a separate manual Home while arming in manual mode; it did not update mission Home. The source now allows a visible 30 m mission start area and uses SA held at mission start to capture the current GPS fix as Home for that run. It requires the captured point and route to the first waypoint to remain inside the boundary, records the captured Home in the event log and session manifest, and restores the planned Home after the run. This source change has not been compiled, flashed, or confirmed on the buoy.
- The main water-test boot (`5EBD29D6`) has 1,474 standalone telemetry rows from 21:51:20 to 22:16:01 UTC. Its first recorded battery voltage is 12.470 V; the minimum is 7.967 V at 22:13:48.5 UTC while drawing 12.095 A; the last reading is 11.813 V. A later boot (`9047A066`) reports roughly 3.49 V without motors. These readings support investigating a depleted pack, but cannot establish a calibrated 0% state of charge by themselves.
- Motor-group energy is integrated from adjacent valid voltage/current samples no more than 2.5 seconds apart. Simultaneous motor operation prevents a defensible independent energy contribution for each motor. The workbook retains each motor's command/PWM so a later controlled calibration can estimate that.
- Daniel's September 26 project conversation records a 12 V, 2,600 mAh (2.6 Ah) rechargeable Li-ion pack, or 31.2 Wh at its nominal voltage. The main field-test boot's valid adjacent samples integrate to 0.7713 Ah and 8.7502 Wh. If that same pack was used, the covered samples represent 29.7% of rated charge and 28.0% of nominal energy. This is logged energy use, not remaining state of charge. Sampling gaps, the pack identity, and current-sensor calibration need checking before explaining the reported exhaustion or estimating each motor's independent drain.
- `range_mm` is the underwater distance reading; do not label it as total water depth without confirming sensor orientation and calibration. TDS and turbidity remain diagnostic volts pending wet calibration.
