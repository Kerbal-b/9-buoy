## Photographing Tips

- Photograph labels straight-on in good light.
- Include a ruler or coin for scale where size matters.
- Take pictures before hiding boards under wiring.
- Photograph connector order and wire colors at both ends.
- Do not assume red is always positive or black is always ground; record the measured connection.
- Remove or obscure Wi-Fi passwords, serial numbers, addresses, and other private identifiers before publishing photographs.
